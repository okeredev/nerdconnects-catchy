NERD Registration Requirements — Complete Document Checklist

📅 September 25, 2026 ⏱️ 6 min read ✍️ NerdConnects Team

One of the most common reasons for NERD registration delays is incomplete or incorrectly formatted documents. This guide provides a comprehensive checklist of every document you need, with exact specifications so your registration goes through without any hitches.

📋 Quick Checklist Summary

You need: 1) Thesis/Dissertation PDF, 2) Passport photo, 3) Valid ID, 4) Certification page, 5) Abstract, 6) Payment proof. Some institutions also require an authorization letter.

Required Documents for NERD Registration

1. Thesis, Dissertation, or Project Report

This is the main document being submitted to the NERD repository. Requirements:

  • Format: PDF only (not Word, not scanned images)
  • Quality: Must be clearly readable with properly formatted text
  • Content: Complete thesis including title page, abstract, all chapters, references, and appendices
  • File size: Should not exceed 50MB (compress if necessary)
  • Title page: Must include the thesis title, your full name, institution, degree, department, and year

2. Passport Photograph

  • Format: JPG or PNG
  • Background: White or light-coloured background preferred
  • Recency: Must be taken within the last 6 months
  • Quality: Clear, well-lit, front-facing
  • Attire: Formal or semi-formal (no headgear unless for religious reasons)

3. Valid Government-Issued ID

Any one of the following:

  • National Identification Number (NIN) slip or card
  • International passport (data page)
  • Voter's card (PVC)
  • Driver's licence

⚠️ Important: Name Matching

The name on your ID must match the name on your thesis title page and NERD registration form. Any discrepancy can cause delays. If your names differ, provide a sworn affidavit or change-of-name document.

4. Certification / Approval Page

  • The signed page from your institution confirming your thesis has been approved
  • Must bear the signatures of your supervisor(s) and Head of Department
  • Must be clearly legible — avoid blurry scans
  • Format: PDF or clear JPG/PNG scan

5. Abstract

  • A standalone copy of your thesis abstract
  • Should be no more than 300 words
  • Must include the thesis title and your name
  • Format: PDF

6. Payment Proof

  • Receipt or screenshot of your NERD registration fee payment
  • Must clearly show the amount paid and transaction reference
  • Format: PDF, JPG, or PNG

7. Additional Documents (If Required)

Some institutions may also require:

  • Authorization letter — A letter from your institution authorising the NERD submission
  • Dedication page — If applicable to your thesis
  • Table of contents — As a standalone document (rarely required)

Common Document Issues & How to Avoid Them

  • Blurry scans — Use a scanner app like CamScanner or Adobe Scan for clear document captures
  • Wrong file format — Always convert Word documents to PDF before uploading
  • Missing pages — Double-check that your thesis PDF includes ALL pages from title page to appendices
  • Name mismatches — Ensure consistency across all documents
  • Unsigned certification page — The page must be signed; unsigned pages are rejected
  • Oversized files — Use online PDF compressors if your thesis exceeds the size limit
