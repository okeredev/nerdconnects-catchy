How to Register for NERD in Nigeria — Step-by-Step Guide 2026

📅 September 20, 2026 ⏱️ 10 min read ✍️ NerdConnects Team

Registering for NERD (Nigeria Education Repository & Databank) is a crucial step in your academic journey. Whether you're submitting a Master's thesis, PhD dissertation, or final-year project report, this guide will walk you through every step of the NERD registration process in 2026.

⏱️ How Long Does NERD Registration Take?

Before You Start: What You'll Need

Before beginning the NERD registration process, make sure you have the following documents ready:

  • Your thesis, dissertation, or project report in PDF format
  • Recent passport photograph (white background preferred)
  • Valid government-issued ID (NIN, voter's card, international passport, or driver's licence)
  • Certification/approval page — the signed page from your institution confirming your work
  • Abstract page of your thesis
  • Authorization letter from your institution (if required)
  • Payment proof — evidence of your NERD registration fee payment

For a complete list of requirements with detailed specifications, see our NERD Requirements Checklist.

Tips for a Smooth Registration

  • Double-check your thesis title — Ensure it matches exactly on all documents
  • Use clear scans — Blurry or unreadable documents will delay processing
  • Keep your phone accessible — The team may call to clarify details
  • Don't wait until deadline — Start your NERD registration early to avoid last-minute stress
  • Save your confirmation — Keep your NERD registration receipt safe for your records

Common Mistakes to Avoid

These are the most frequent reasons NERD registrations get delayed:

  • Submitting a thesis without the signed certification page
  • Using a blurry or passport-sized photograph that doesn't meet requirements
  • Name mismatch between thesis, ID, and registration form
  • Incomplete payment or using incorrect payment reference
  • Uploading documents in wrong formats (e.g., Word instead of PDF)
